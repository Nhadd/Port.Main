Leonard S. Dacpano / Personal field notes

Starting with the basics

At STI Alaminos, I learned programming foundations and developed my skills through hands-on projects and activities. Building applications gave me a way to connect classroom lessons to something I could actually test and use.

Getting there together

My classmates and I helped each other understand lessons, finish projects, and work through coding problems. Their support made learning easier, and taught me to be a better team player. Sharing knowledge, being patient, and asking questions became part of the process.

Learning grows when you share what you know and stay open to what others can teach you.
